## v0.6.0 (2026-11-19)

- The tooltip lists the outlets in a fixed order, Vendor, Market, then Disenchant with its
  materials, and shows one net price for each in a single right-hand column, with notes in
  grey beside each label.
- Ore and cloth tooltips show what one unit nets smelted into bars or woven into bolts
  and sold. A bar or bolt with a thin market is never marked best.
- A stack's worth now uses the best outlet's net price instead of the listed wall price.
- The Sell page lists its bag groups in a fixed order, and always shows the auction house
  and Vendor groups, even when empty.
- The Sell page and the disenchant log now include items in the reagent bag.
